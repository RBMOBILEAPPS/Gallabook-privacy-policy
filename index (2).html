Privacy Policy

GALLA BOOK — Business & Inventory Management

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GALLA BOOK handles business records, inventory, billing information, Google Sign-In, Google Drive backup and restore, advertising information, permissions, and third-party services.

Overview

GALLA BOOK is an Android business-management application developed by RBAPPS. It helps shop owners manage products, stock, sales, bills, customers, and related business records.

GALLA BOOK stores operational business information locally on your device to provide app features. If you choose to connect Google Drive and use the backup or restore feature, the app transfers the backup data to or from your Google account's app-specific Google Drive storage.

Your choice: Google Drive backup is used to support the backup/restore feature you choose to use. Google Sign-In and Google Drive services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

1. Business information handled by the app

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, GALLA BOOK may process and store business records such as:

  • Product names, product codes, categories, prices, and stock quantities
  • Sales, purchase, billing, invoice, and transaction records
  • Customer or supplier details that you choose to enter, which may include names, phone numbers, addresses, or balances
  • Expenses, payments, account balances, and other business records supported by the app
  • App preferences and settings needed to operate the application

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to enter and manage information about your customers, suppliers, employees, and other people in the app.

2. Local storage and processing

Business records are stored in the app's local storage/database on your Android device so that you can use GALLA BOOK. RBAPPS does not operate a separate RBAPPS business-record server for the app's normal local record-keeping features.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it using app features, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. The availability of Android device backup or transfer may depend on Android settings and the app's backup configuration.

3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup

GALLA BOOK offers Google Sign-In to connect your Google account for Google Drive backup and restore features. When you choose to connect and use these features:

  • Google authentication is used to identify and authorize the Google account you select.
  • The app requests the Google Drive access needed for its app-specific backup feature.
  • When you run or enable backup, the app uploads the supported app backup data to Google Drive storage associated with the app.
  • When you choose restore, the app downloads the selected available backup from Google Drive and uses it to restore app data on your device.
  • The backup may contain the business records listed in Section 1 and other database information required to restore the app.

Google Drive app-specific storage (commonly referred to as appDataFolder, when that scope is used) is hidden from the normal Google Drive file list and is intended for data used by the connected application. The exact backup contents depend on the app version and backup implementation.

Google processes sign-in and Drive requests under Google's privacy policy and terms. RBAPPS does not use your Google password. Never share your Google password or private authentication credentials with anyone.

Only connect an account you control or are authorized to use. You can stop using the Drive feature by disconnecting or no longer using Google Sign-In, subject to the controls available in the app and your Google Account.

4. Information shared with third parties

RBAPPS does not sell your business records or customer information. Information is transferred to Google when you choose Google Sign-In or use Google Drive backup and restore. The transfer is necessary to provide those features.

If you use Android sharing or export features, the information or file you select is shared with the destination app or service you choose. That recipient's privacy practices apply to the information you share.

5. Advertising

GALLA BOOK may display advertisements using Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. When ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising-related information to request and deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent invalid traffic and fraud, perform attribution, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law, ads may be personalized, limited, or non-personalized. Advertising providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Permissions and device access

GALLA BOOK requests device permissions or access only as needed for features you use. Depending on the installed version and enabled features, this may include:

  • Internet/network access: for Google Sign-In, Google Drive backup/restore, advertisements, and related online services.
  • Notifications: if the app uses notifications on your device and you allow them.
  • Files or media access: if you choose to export, import, or share files, subject to Android's system picker and permission model.

The exact permissions can vary by Android version, app version, and third-party SDKs included in the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Local business records: remain on your device until deleted through app controls, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.
  • Google Drive backups: remain in Google Drive until removed by an available in-app deletion feature, if provided, or through applicable Google account/app-data controls. Uninstalling the app should not be treated as confirmation that every cloud backup has been deleted.
  • Advertising and diagnostics information: is handled and retained by Google and other applicable service providers under their policies.

Before uninstalling GALLA BOOK, changing devices, or clearing app data, make sure you have a usable backup of any business information you need to keep. Always verify that a backup can be restored before relying on it as your only copy.

8. Security

GALLA BOOK uses local app storage and Google authentication/Drive services to provide its features. RBAPPS takes reasonable steps in the app's design to protect information, but no device, software, storage system, or network service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep your Google account secure, and do not share backup access with people you do not trust.

9. Children's privacy

GALLA BOOK is a business-management utility and is not designed specifically for children. It is intended for people who are able to manage business records and use the app in accordance with applicable laws. RBAPPS does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a child-directed account system.

10. Third-party services

Depending on the app version and features you use, GALLA BOOK may integrate services such as:

  • Google Sign-In and Google Play services
  • Google Drive API for app-specific backup and restore
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

These services are provided by third parties and their processing is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

RBAPPS may update this Privacy Policy when GALLA BOOK's features, legal requirements, SDKs, or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
